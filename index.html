About this version

Golden Dawn Western Tattva, not Hindu Tattva. This app contains only the Golden Dawn Western Tattva set. It does not contain any other Tattva system. A later version may cover a different system, separately.

The cards are shown on plain white, as approved. No flashing background colours have been added, because no flashing background colour was verified card by card in the research notes.

This is a study and practice aid. It makes no promise of a result, and it is not medical or mental health advice.

Method, in brief

The method below is a summary in plain words from the research notes. It is not a guided script, and no vision content is supplied for any single card.

  1. Choose one card, a single or a double.
  2. Gaze at the symbol, and hold its image in mind.
  3. Enlarge the symbol in imagination to the size of a door.
  4. Pass through the door in imagination, and explore the scene beyond it.
  5. Return through the door in imagination.
  6. Record the working in your journal, below, on this device only.

For a double, the large symbol is the main element, and the smaller symbol inside it is the sub element. In the research notes, the sub symbol is described as about one fifth of the area of the main symbol.
